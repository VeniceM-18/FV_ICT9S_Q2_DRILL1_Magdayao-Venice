🥋 Karate Beginner's Guide



Basic Rules

  1. Wear proper uniform during training.
  2. Respect your Sensei and training partners.
  3. Bow before entering and leaving the dojo.
  4. Do not use karate techniques carelessly or outside training.

Basic Techniques

  1. Oi-zuki - Straight punch
  2. Gedan-barai Downward block
  3. Kakato-geri - Axe kick
  4. Mawashi-geri - Roundhouse kick

Basic Equipment

  1. Karate Gi
  2. Belt
  3. Gloves
  4. Shin Guards
